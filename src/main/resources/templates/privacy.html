Privacy Policy

Last Updated:

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide a better educational experience. This includes:

  • Student Data: Name, Index Number, Class, Profile Picture.
  • Parent/Guardian Data: Full Name, Phone Number, Location/Area of Stay, Email Address.
  • Academic Data: Attendance records, Exam scores, Quizzes, and Report Cards.
  • Financial Data: Fee payments and receipts.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information strictly for school administration. This includes marking attendance, generating report cards, processing fees, and sending important SMS/Email notifications (like fee reminders or student pickup alerts).

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data to third parties. Your information is only accessible to authorized school administrators and the specific teachers assigned to your child's class. Third-party services (like Mobile Money gateways or SMS providers) only receive the minimum information necessary to process a transaction or send a message.

4. Data Security

We take security seriously. All passwords are encrypted using BCrypt hashing. Payment transactions are processed securely. However, no system is 100% immune to breaches, and the school must ensure that physical access to devices is restricted.

5. Your Rights

Parents have the right to request corrections to their child's data. If you believe your data is inaccurate, please contact the school administration directly.
